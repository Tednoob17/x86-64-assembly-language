	## 0.2 Interactive Mechanics
	This book integrated interactive quizzes, test it in clicking "Start".

	When your response to question is incorrect you can choose to either rety the quizz or
	see the correct answers. It interesting and encouraged to retry the quiz until you get 100%.
	Note that after respond correctly to quizz you cannot retry it.

	You can also take note, select text and add your thoughts about it. 
	Once text selected, click the button and write a optionnal comment.

	> **Note:** your highlights will disappear if we change the content that you've highlighted. 
	Also, your highlights are stored as a cookie. If you block cookies or change browsers,

## 0.0 Why Assembly?

We choose assembly to provide a clear introduction to low-level programming and to explain how computers work. Understanding assembly helps with debugging, reverse engineering, vulnerability research, and writing performant low-level code. It also bridges the gap between beginners who rely on copy/paste and experienced engineers who understand system behaviour.

This material is intended to give readers a solid foundation so they can adapt to changing technologies.

## 0.1 An interactive experience

This book is an experimental project inspired by *x86-64 Assembly Language Programming with Ubuntu* by Ed Jorgensen and the Cognitive Engineering Lab at Brown University.

## 0.2 Interactive mechanics

The book integrates interactive quizzes. When a quiz is incorrect you can retry or view the correct answers — retrying until you get 100% is encouraged. After answering correctly a quiz cannot be retried.

You can also highlight text and add optional notes. Highlights are stored in cookies and will be lost if the content changes or if you change browser or block cookies.

> **Note:** Highlights are stored in cookies; blocking cookies or switching browsers will remove them.
